## 🚀 What is CET Counsellor?

**CET Counsellor** is an AI agent that helps Maharashtra engineering students navigate the most confusing part of their academic journey — **MHT-CET and JEE CAP admissions**.

Every year, lakhs of students appear for MHT-CET. But the CAP process? Genuinely confusing. Multiple rounds, 13 seat categories, different documents for each, cutoffs that change every year.

**I went through this myself. So I built the tool I wish I had.**

Ask it anything — in **English, Marathi, or Hinglish**. 🗣️

---

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **AI Agent** — Powered by structured Sanity knowledge base
- 🏫 **201+ Colleges** — Pune and Mumbai region
- 📊 **555+ Branches** — CS, IT, AI & Data Science, ML, Cyber Security
- 📋 **All 13 Categories** — OPEN, OBC, SC, ST, VJNT, EWS, TFWS, PWD, Defence, Ex-servicemen
- 🔄 **CAP Round 1, 2 & 3** — Official 2026-27 cutoff data
- 💬 **Multilingual** — English, Marathi, Hinglish
- 📱 **Mobile First** — Works perfectly on any device
- 💾 **Saved Conversations** — Browser-saved chat history

## 💡 Why I Built This

I'm a first-year CSE student at VIT Pune (MHT-CET 98.46 percentile). I went through the entire CAP process this year and know how stressful and confusing it is.

**The worst part?** Scattered information. CET Cell PDFs are 1500+ pages. No one has time for that.

So I built CET Counsellor — an AI that reads those PDFs so students don't have to.

**Built entirely on mobile. No laptop. No terminal. Just determination.** 💪
